[ 01 / THE PROBLEM ]

Why Stock AI Models Fail

Most financial machine learning models collapse in real trading due to two critical engineering traps.

TRAP 1: THE COPY-PASTE IDENTITY BUG
Predicting Raw Stock Prices (P_t+30)

Because raw stock prices change slowly day-to-day, naive AI models simply output yesterday's price as tomorrow's forecast. It shows a fake "98% R² accuracy" on paper, but has zero real predictive value.

TRAP 2: LOOKAHEAD DATA LEAKAGE
Overlapping Future Data in Training

Standard random train/test splits accidentally let future 30-day stock returns leak into past training sets. The model memorizes future knowledge and fails completely in production.

[ 02 / HOW I SOLVED IT ]

Stationary Targets & Risk Cones

I rebuilt the learning formulation and validation structure to ensure true out-of-sample edge.

1. PREDICTING 30-DAY FORWARD LOG RETURN
y_t = ln( P_{t+30} / P_t )

Forces the model to predict trend and momentum rather than raw nominal prices, achieving statistical stationarity across 26 equities.

2. MULTI-QUANTILE RISK ENVELOPES (PINBALL LOSS)
P10 Downside Floor + P50 Median + P90 Upside Ceiling

Replaced dangerous single price guesses with probabilistic uncertainty envelopes, giving investors downside protection and realistic targets.

3. PURGED & EMBARGOED CROSS-VALIDATION
30-Day Purge Gap + 30-Day Embargo Buffer

Guarantees 100% leak-free evaluation by removing overlapping return labels and killing autoregressive spillover.
